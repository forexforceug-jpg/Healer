About me

I was called by my ancestors as a young man and spent years training under my mentor in the Busoga region of Uganda. I do not promise miracles. I listen, I throw the bones, and I tell you what the ancestors show me — whether it is what you want to hear or not.

Most of the people who come to me are struggling with love. A partner who left. A relationship that feels cursed. A heart that cannot move on. I help where I can, and I am honest when I cannot.
